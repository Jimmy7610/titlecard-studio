WHY THESE FILES ARE IN THE REPOSITORY
-------------------------------------
The visual regression suite has to render the same pixels on a laptop and on a
CI runner. Fetching a face from fonts.googleapis.com at test time makes the
screenshots depend on the network and on whatever version Google is currently
serving, and falling back to a system stack makes them depend on which fonts the
machine happens to have installed. These two subsets are served from the test
fixture instead, so every visual test renders against one known face.

They are used by the test suite only. The application still loads its faces from
Google Fonts at runtime; nothing here ships to users.
